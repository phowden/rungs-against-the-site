#fey 

Fey resembling human women, but with fox tails and a wooden hollow in their backs. They're said to bring luck *good or bad* and are sought after by ship captains as lucky charms for their crew.

[[Huldra]] must sustain themselves on the blood of sentient creatures.

Many [[Huldra]] try to pass as normal human women by disguising their tail and their wooden hollow.

# Origins
Legends tell that [[Huldra]] were created by [[Troll Witches]] to lure humans through seduction. They're said to be corrupted [[Dryad|Dryads]], replacing their reliance on nature with a reliance on blood. [[Huldra]] hate this story, and most deny it whenever it's brought up.

Recently, [[Rotta of Gungnir]] has found evidence that this tale of [[Troll Witches]] is not accurate. She found evidence that the origin of the [[Huldra]] is possibly related to the ancient [[Serpent Empire]], based on references she found to rituals. The evidence suggests they created the [[Huldra]] to battle their enemies in [[Azlanti]].

# Possibility of a Cure
[[Rotta of Gungnir]] is searching for a way to reverse the rituals she believes were used by the [[Serpent Empire]] to create the [[Huldra]]. The ritual separates [[Dryad|Dryads]] from nature & trees. 

She hopes that in reversing the ritual she can cure the [[Huldra]] of their "temporary condition."

The cure possibly resides on the island of [[Lilywhite]].
