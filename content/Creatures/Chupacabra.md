Blood sucking creature, they can "chupar" to suck the blood of a creature they've pinned and drain their life. They can stun creatures with their piercing shriek.

Magical beast, not undead. A predator that attacks from hiding. Preys on the weakest. Combines the stealth and dexterity of a mountain lion with the flight capabilities of a bat.

There's a known [[Chupacabra]] on the Red Mountain on [[Smuggler's Shiv]], known as the "Red Mountain Devil."
