A cured [[Huldra]] becomes a [[Calventrian]], no longer cursed to drink living blood but also not restored to their original [[Dryad]] state.

[[Rotta of Gungnir]] was the first [[Huldra]] in history, transformed when she (along with the [[Rungs Against the Seven]]) broke her curse on the isle of [[Smuggler's Shiv]] in the temple of [[Zura]].
