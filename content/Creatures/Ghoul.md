Lots of types of [[Ghoul|ghouls]]
- Normal ghouls, bog standard
	- Live underground, dig tunnels, etc
	- NOT mindless, same range of intelligence as living beings
	- Become a ghoul by being bitten or getting ghoul fever
	- Retain your memory, but gain an eternal hunger
- Biome based [[Ghoul|ghouls]]
	- Aquatic, swamp, *jungle*, etc
	- All inflict [[Ghoul]] fever

Strengths / Weaknesses
- Weak to [[Positive Energy]], absorb [[Negative Energy]]
	- But more resistant to [[Positive Energy]] than others
- Cause disease that turns into a [[Ghoul]]
	- Incubation period is one day, show no symptoms
	- Then every day you struggle to survive, the stronger you are the longer you last
- Cause paralysis
	- Elves are immune (and half-elves)
	- Cannot be dispelled/resisted once paralyzed, just takes time
- Retain skills & capabilities from their previous lives
- Exude a disgusting stench that makes you sick to your stomach

There are powerful [[Ghoul|ghouls]] called [[Ghoul|ghasts]] that arise from more powerful creatures. The paralysis of ghasts *does* effect elves.

The transformation to a [[Ghoul]] can go wrong, turn into a [[Ghoul|festrog]] instead
- They are more savage than average [[Ghoul|ghouls]]
- Have a harder time controlling their hunger, very feral
- Gain direct sustenance from feeding on the living
- Often covered in pustules that can burst (or they can cause them to burst) to infect their attackers with *Filth Fever*
