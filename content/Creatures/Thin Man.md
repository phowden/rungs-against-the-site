Fey creatures, long part of legend. Only described as occurring on the island [[Nuat]]

Lived on the periphery of human society forever

Their thin frames and coloring help them blend in with sugar cane fields, lets them avoid detection by local farmers.

They have very few bones, they consist almost entirely of cartilage

Very close connection with the earth, gives them natural immunity to poison

They like to observe their human counterparts from the shadows, they sometimes catch local farmers at night and murder them... And feast on the remains.
