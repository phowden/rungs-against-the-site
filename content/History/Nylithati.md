Was possibly the cleric/chaplain of the [[Thrune's Fang]]. She turned the crew to cannibalism upon their crash, she discovered the [[Serpent Empire]] evil cathedral under the ground. Was turned into a [[Ghoul]] and became a cleric of [[Ydersius]].


The [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] fought and killed [[Nylithati|Mother Thrunefang]] in the [[Ghoul]] warrens under the camp of the [[Thrunefang Tribe|Cannibal tribe]]. [[Trinica]] killed her in three swift strokes, severing and arm and her tongue, and finally beheading her!
