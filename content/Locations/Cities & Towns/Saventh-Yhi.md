Ancient [[Azlanti]] city built on top of the dead [[Serpent Empire]] city of [[Ilmurea]].

Translates to "[[Savneth]]'s Grave"

An [[Azlanti]] hero went to a [[Serpentfolk]] city and beheaded [[Ydersius]]
- This ended the war between the [[Azlanti]] and the [[Serpentfolk]]
- Then the [[Azlanti]] built a city on top of the [[Serpentfolk]]

- [[Saventh-Yhi]] known as a golden city, holds incredible gems/gold/riches
- Said to be in the middle of the [[Mwangi Expanse]]
- There's no record that the [[Azlanti]] empire expanded that far

The city is an origin of a cult of [[Zura]]

Connections found on the island of [[Smuggler's Shiv]], where the cult was drawn
