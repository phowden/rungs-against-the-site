City in [[Hermea]]

They give contracts to freebooters to protect the harbor, they board every ship that approachs to inspect the ship & gather reasons for visits.

[[Promise]] is a city built based on a utopian vision to create the perfect world and the perfect human. It is guided & ruled over by an ancient golden [[Dragon]] [[Mengkare]] who founded & brought the most promising humans to live there.

Separated into 12 districts based on aspects of [[Aroden]]. Each district is dedicated to a certain production or artistry. The city is known for great artisans and the work they produce.

[[Mengkare]] considers religion a distraction, but values trade relations, so the only allowed temple in the city is to [[Abadar]].
