Town on the [[Arcadian Ocean]], atop a steep crag with a lighthouse (The Devil's Eye, the Eye of [[Asmodeus]])

The villagers in the town were being turned to [[Ulat-kini|Skum]] by an [[Azlanti]] altar they knew as "Wedding Rock" and a tradition of conceiving children on it...

The sorceror [[Gerlach]] sailed to [[Nal Kashel]] in an attempt to find a cure, but he somehow made it worse when he disappeared.
