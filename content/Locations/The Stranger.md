A planet in the [[Golarion]] solar system

Rumoured to ALSO have life on it! Strange life, ghostly apparitions, skinless hunters. Populations worshiping the great [[Old Ones]] and the [[Dark Tapestry]].

One of the only planets in the system without any portals to it from any other.
