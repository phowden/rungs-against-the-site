Captain of the [[Kraken's Spite]]. 

Brought the [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] onboard at the behest of the [[White Estrid]] in order to help [[Rotta of Gungnir]] find the [[Huldra]] cure.

Brought on the [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] as crew after they proved themselves on [[Nal Kashel]].

Was ensorcelled by [[Atli Gudronson|Atli]] and drove the [[Kraken's Spite]] aground on [[Smuggler's Shiv]].
