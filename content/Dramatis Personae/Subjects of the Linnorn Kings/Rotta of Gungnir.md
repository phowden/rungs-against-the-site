[[Huldra]] sailor on the [[Kraken's Spite]]. 

She sailed with the [[Kraken's Spite]] to [[Vallenhall]] looking for the possibility of a cure to her affliction. She is now seeking further clues in [[Smuggler's Shiv]] based on references to the ritual that created her kind.

She had agreed to take a role as advisor to the [[White Estrid]], and is stretching the king's patience by requesting leave to sail to [[Smuggler's Shiv]]. As such she is being accompanied by the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] for additional assurance for the [[White Estrid]].

[[Rotta of Gungnir|Rotta]] has been working on a cure for the curse of the [[Huldra]] since she was very young. She has a bunch of (maps, scrolls, texts) pertaining to the cause. She believes it is a [[Serpent Empire]] ritual, she has a [[Serpent Empire]] scripture that tells of a temple on the island.
