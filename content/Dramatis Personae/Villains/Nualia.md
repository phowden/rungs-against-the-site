Half-angel daughter of [[Father Tobyn]].

Allied with [[Goblin|Goblins]] to raid [[Sandpoint]] after being (in her eyes) betrayed by [[Delek Viskanta|Delek]], her father and the citizens of [[Sandpoint]]. 

Turned to [[Lamashtu]] for power and transformed into a clawed half-angel half-demon half-human monster...

Was defeated by the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] in the temple under [[Thistletop]].
