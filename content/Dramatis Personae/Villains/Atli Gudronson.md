Outwardly the cook & quartermaster of the [[Kraken's Spite]], secretly a [[Serpentfolk]] shape-shifter.

Orchestrated the shipwreck of the [[Kraken's Spite]] on [[Smuggler's Shiv]] in order to prepare a [[Serpentfolk]] ritual. Ensorcelled [[Kjell]] into being his accomplice before preparing to sacrifice him to achieve his ritual.
