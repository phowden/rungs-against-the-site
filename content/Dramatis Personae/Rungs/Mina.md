A halfling oracle, [[Mina]] was adopted by [[Melia Goldtide]] and [[Dranos Goldtide]] after coming to [[Sandpoint]]. She was struck by lightning on her coming-of-age event on [[Chopper's Island]], and soon after began manifesting powers.

Member of the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] and originally a scribe in [[Sandpoint]] working for the [[Goldtide Family]] business.

[[Mina]] is developing her martial abilities as the (unexpected?) strongest member of the [[Rungs Against the Seven]].

In the [[First World]] ([[Hither]]) [[Mina]] conned a drink from [[Trinket, Bauble, and Charm]] which caused her to grow a bee stinger.

# [[Empyreal Lord]]
[[Mina]] has connections to the [[Empyreal Lord]] [[Ashava]].
