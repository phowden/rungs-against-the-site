Local wise-woman from [[Sandpoint]] and friend to the [[Rungs Against the Seven]], especially [[Lotus]].

Manifested to the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] when they were in the [[Vault of Thorns]] and healed their wounds, weakening her greatly.

Contacted the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] again in their dreams 27 days later ([[Rule of Three]]!!!) to get them to use the *Sending Stone* she gave them.
