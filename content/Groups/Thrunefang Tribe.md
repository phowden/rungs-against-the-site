Tribe of cannibals on [[Smuggler's Shiv]] that worship [[Nylithati|Mother Thrunefang]]. They are the descendants of the [[Cheliax|Chelaxian]] crew of the warship [[Thrune's Fang]], which shipwrecked some 70 years before the [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] discovered the tribe.

They sacrifice members of their tribe to [[Nylithati|Mother Thrunefang]] as a form of worship.

The [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] killed their [[Witch]] leader Molikatna & their barbarian leader Klorak, [[Skar]] became the de facto leade as a result.
