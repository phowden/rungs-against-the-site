Trade organization with a far reach across Avistan & Garund. 

What sets them apart from other organizations is their sheer size. Valued in the hundreds of millions of gold pieces.

They're also known to be particularly ruthless, they'll do anything to grow their wealth. There are stories of them selling weapons to both sides of wars, where they may have had a hand in starting the war.

They're loyal to no government, they have members from all nationalities.
