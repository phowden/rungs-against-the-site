Society of adventurers

They're all about uncovering or preserving ancient knowledge, the greatest honor is to have their findings published by the society.

There's some secrecy, rumors that they're ruled over the the "decamvirate" who are ten individuals that never appear in public. They appear masked to all their council meetings. They want to avoid being unduly influenced, or tortured/killed...
