Assassin's guild dedicated to the deity [[Achaekek]], originated in [[Rahadoum]] before being kicked out for worshipping [[Achaekek]].

Headquartered on Mediogalti. Originated around 2558 [[Aroden Reckoning|AR]], around 1000 years ago.

- Their contract generally remains in effect forever, until they complete it
- They have "contingency contracts" that triggers on conditions other than simply assasination
- They *don't* kill outside of their contracts

Wear red and black armor with insectoid masks

Fight with sawtooth sabers, with a fighting style that emulates the attacking pose of a mantis
