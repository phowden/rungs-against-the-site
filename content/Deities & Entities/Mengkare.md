Golden [[Dragon]], ruler of the city of [[Promise]] in [[Hermea]].

Spent centuries among the human race in disguises, saw the good & bad in the race. He developed a vision that if you picked just the best and provided eveything for them then it would propel the human race forward (more than [[Aroden]]'s teachings).

Eventually he sent invites to select people, of all genders and ages, but all humans. First invited builders to build [[Promise]] in 12 districts (based on [[Aroden]]'s aspect) and dedicated to different products.

They all had to agree that [[Mengkare]] makes the big decisions, he decides the major projects they work on. There's rumors he even chooses their spouses, to produce the most promising offspring.
