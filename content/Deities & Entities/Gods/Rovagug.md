One of the original deities born when [[Pharasma]] willed existence into being. Either was born from the gnawing, devouring fear she felt stepping off [[The Seal]] or [[Rovagug]] **was** the devouring fear.

# Defeat and Imprisonment
A coalition of many Gods teamed up to defeat [[Rovagug]], including:
- [[Asmodeus]]
- [[Abadar]]
- [[Sarenrae]]
- [[Calistria]]
- [[Torag]]
- [[Desna]]
- [[Pharasma]]
- [[Gorum]]
- [[Zon-Kuthon|Dou-Bral]]

More gods participated, these are just the ones whose actions are most remembered:
- [[Calistria]] distracted [[Rovagug]] to give the other gods opportunity to plan
- [[Torag]] and [[Gorum]] forged the [[Dead Vault]]
- [[Abadar]] created the lock & key to the [[Dead Vault]] that only [[Asmodeus]] can turn
- [[Zon-Kuthon|Dou-Bral]] built the [[Star Towers]] that impale [[Rovagug]] and strengthen the seals of the [[Dead Vault]]
- [[Sarenrae]] sliced open [[Golarion]] to embed the [[Dead Vault]] deep within the core of the planet and later sealed the rift
- [[Asmodeus]] bound [[Rovagug]] with the key created by [[Abadar]]
