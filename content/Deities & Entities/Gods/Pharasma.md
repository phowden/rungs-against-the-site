[Wiki Link](https://pathfinderwiki.com/wiki/Pharasma)

 The goddess who shepherds [[Golarion]]'s recently-departed souls to their final reward. Upon death, souls migrate via the [[River of Souls]] to [[Pharasma]]'s [[Boneyard]] in the [[Outer Sphere]], which sits atop an impossibly tall spire that pierces the [[Astral Plane]].

One of the gods who battled and ultimately sealed away [[Rovagug]].
