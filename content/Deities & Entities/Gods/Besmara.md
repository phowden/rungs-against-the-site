Goddess of Pirates, Goddess of the Sea, Pirate Queen

Her ship is the Sea Wraith.

Very few priests, as most pirates are only religious on their death beds.

Believes in a freewheeling lifestyle and flouting society's norms. The only rules she adheres to is the pirate codes. Demands loyalty to the captain and crew. Encourages followers to sieze what you want regardless of who owns it, fight dirty, sow chaos.

Depicted as a women wearing a pirates outfit, loose fitting clothes and perpetually wind blown hair.

Counts [[Cayden Cailian]] and [[Gozreh]] as her allies, [[Asmodeus]] as an enemy. Known to raid the plane of Hell and other lawful planes.
