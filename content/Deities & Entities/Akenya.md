The [[Dryad]] protector of [[Smuggler's Shiv]]. A "Hama[[Dryad]]" that casts spells as a **level 20 [[Druid]]**.

For countless years she has kept the power of the [[Crimson Chorus]] from spreading and taking over, which sapped nearly all her strength.

When the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] defeated the [[Crimson Chorus]] she was finally able to do more than just keep the [[Crimson Chorus]]' power dampened.

Told [[Rotta of Gungnir]] the truth about her new status as a [[Calventrian]].

Removed the *Feeblemind* spell upon [[Io]].
