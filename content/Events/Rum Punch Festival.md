A celebration held in the [[Shackles]] on the island of [[Lilywhite]]. Commemorates the massive escape of slaves from a [[Cheliax|Chelaxian]] slaveship. [[Sargava|Sargavan]] slave escape from the port of [[Crown's End]].

The crews of all three ships got roaring drunk despite drinking a normal amount. The crew fell asleep leaving their keys within grasp of the slaves.

The slaves took this as a sign that [[Cayden Cailian]] had intervened.

The slaves followed 5 birds to find a ship that they took to sail to [[Shackles]].

The celebration is named for the power of the rum that gave the slaves their freedom.
