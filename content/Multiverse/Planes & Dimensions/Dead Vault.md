The demi-plane prison of the mad god [[Rovagug]]. Located in the core of the planet [[Golarion]]. Created and sealed by a coalition of gods:
- [[Torag]] and [[Gorum]] forged it
- [[Abadar]] created the lock & key
- [[Zon-Kuthon|Dou-Bral]] built the [[Star Towers]] strengthen the seals 
- [[Sarenrae]] sliced open [[Golarion]] to embed it within the planets core
- [[Asmodeus]] turned the key to lock [[Rovagug]] within
