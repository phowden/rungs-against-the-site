Three parts of the same key, the secret to [[Gemma Redclaw]]'s treasure. Possibly combined to create a flute-like instrument. Broken into three pieces by three pirates: Harkam, Dahoath, and Lanteri. The last remaining pirate alive inherits the other two keys.

Sought by [[Varossa Lanteri]] & the crew of the [[Magpie Prince|Magpie Princess]]. 

Locations of the [[Three Reasons to Live]]:
1) In a pegleg, of the captain hung from [[Blackwarn's Gallows]] (Harkam)
	1) Recovered by the [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] 
2) In the wreck of the Saber's Kiss, protected by a [[Brine Dragon]] Kelezar (Dahoath)
	1) Recovered by the [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]]
3) Varvel's Folly, the island Varvel Lanteri was marooned on (Lanteri)
