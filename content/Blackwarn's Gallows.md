Arch near [[Besmara's Throne]] where a captain was hung after killing a priest of [[Besmara]]. The peg leg of this captain is one of the [[Three Reasons to Live]].
- Captain Raheme

Three giant anchor chains hang from the arch, with smaller chains hanging down with three corpses attached to them.

[[Blackwarn's Gallows]] is shaded by perpetual black storm clouds, though no lightning seems to be striking.

## Captain Raheme
- Captain Raheme cut off his own leg to gain clout with his crew
- He also just... wanted a pegleg...
- He didn't trust anyone to protect his treasures
