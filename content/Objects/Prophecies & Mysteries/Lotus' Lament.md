One of three prophecies found in the hut of [[Bavlorna Blightstraw]], in [[Hither]]. Alongside [[Chalmymokos' Conundrum]] and [[Sazzleru's Spark]].
# Text
> [!quote]
> Decided will be the fate of the land
> When this heptate of signs is near at hand.
> 
> In the house of Zinlun the wizard born
> Through life, unlife, he will Earthfall scorn.
> 
> The drowned one-eyed, to the key he will point
> To unlock where here, shapers’ and dark world are joined
> 
> Island temples of old, where true prophecies were once told,
> The Heir shall gain the Aspect TriFold, allowing time to unfold.
> 
> The half-blood child of stern mother found
> Unravel the first domain of those which are bound.
> 
> Returns land forlorn to the Maker’s vale
> Crushing [[Melaenis]]’ followers without fail
> 
> To another eye, the tower ascends.
> Wyrms at the bottom, wyrms at the end
> 
> A ring both foreign and familiar, to be destroyed
> Before victory can be won and peace be enjoyed.
# Summary
This prophecy describes a sequence of 7 momentous events or signs that must come to pass for the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] to survive and destroy *some unidentified evil or calamity.*

1. A prominent [[Thassilon|Thassilonian]] slaver [[Zinlun]] (or a descendent of [[Zinlun]]) will survive the cataclsymic [[Earthfall]] event through necromancy or possibly lichdom

2. The prophecy [[Chalmymokos' Conundrum]], written by the [[Cyclops]] [[Chalmymokos]] will point the [[Rungs Against the Seven]] towards a location where two planes are converging: the [[First World]] and the [[Dark World]].

> [!warning]
> All events and signs below are not well understood. Further context is most likely required to know the full details.

3. Somewhere in [[Iblydos]] [[W_rm|Worm]] will somehow learn or attain the spell *Threefold Aspect*, which will allow the flow of time to continue.

4. [[Lotus]] will somehow destroy or "unravel" (either the [[First World]] or the [[Dark World]])

5. [[Forfar]] will return in the [[Makers Vale]] in [[Varisia]]. This will crush [[Melaenis]]' followers, but it isn't clear who or what [[Melaenis]] refers to.

6. The [[Eye of Avarice]] will rise from where it's hidden. There will be "wyrms" at the bottom and the end, but it is unclear what this refers to: [[W_rm|Worm]] is the obvious possibility.

7. A ring must be destroyed, but what "foreign and familiar" references is highly unclear. It seems more context is required here.
