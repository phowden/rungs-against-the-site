Map given to the [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] by [[Mersoolian Threce]].

A reference to this map was found under [[Nal Kashel]] inscribed on a set of [[Gugg]]-hide armor.

The location appears to be designed to trap a dragon that tries to get to it's center. Probably the "cradle of the [[Orbs of Dragonkind|Song of the Dawning Star]]" referenced in the note from [[Abdul Alhazred]].

Possibly of [[Azlanti]] origin.
