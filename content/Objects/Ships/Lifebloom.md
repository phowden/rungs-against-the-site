Ship built by [[Kjell|Kjell Snarribrand]] and enchanted by [[Akenya]] on [[Smuggler's Shiv]], enabling the [[Rungs Against the Seven|RATS]] and the surviving crew of the [[Kraken's Spite]] to sail from the island after the shipwreck and destruction of the [[Kraken's Spite]].

It was enchanted by [[Akenya]], an extremely powerful [[Druid]], to mark her thanks for the cleansing of the [[Crimson Chorus]] from [[Smuggler's Shiv]].

The ship transports [[Rotta of Gungnir]], the first [[Calventrian]], on her quest to free the other [[Huldra]] from their curse.
