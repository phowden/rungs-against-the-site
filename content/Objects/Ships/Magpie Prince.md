- Well-constructed [[Cheliax|Chelaxian]] warship
- Keel laid in 4693, completed at Howl of Winter in 4695
	- Mission to safeguard shipping lanes to the Hespereth Strait (Strait of Aroden)
- Captured by pirates in 4699 off the coast of Mediogalti Island
	- Was renamed to Deimavigga (Apostate Devil)
- Succession of Captains had no success until 10 years ago Warvil Lanteri won it in a card game
	- Renamed it the Magpie Prince and cut a bloody wake across the Abendego Bay and into the Inner Sea, where the Magpie Prince’s colors meant death and piracy to ships from across Golarion
- Only snag know is when the Magpie Prince attacked a Taldan merchant ship, which was also the target of the Shark Queen’s crew
	- The two ships raced each other to claim the price, only to find that it was conveying a band of Taldan mercenaries
	- The pirates joined forces, but the pitched battle against the Taldan’s lead to the death of most of the Magpie Prince’s officers and riggers and both, the Shark Queen and the Taldan trader sank
	- Impressed by their respective valor in battle, Warvil Lanteri and the captain of the Shark Queen, Varossa Ia-Tep, joined forces and co-captained the Magpie Prince together from there on
	- Rumors have it that a fiery romance bloomed between the two and briefly afterwards their used their authority at sea to get married, with Varossa taking her husbands last name
