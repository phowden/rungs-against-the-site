An #ulfen ship sailing near the [[Lands of the Linnorm Kings]]. Captained by [[Kjell]] along with his wife [[Aster Ormsdottir|Aster]]. Crewed by around 50 sailors and warriors. 

Previously reavers and pirates, they were sent on a traumatic mission for a [[Linnorm King]]. The crew has since abandoned that life and they now operate as traders. Names of the ships lost on that mission are incorporated into the [[Kraken's Spite]].
